        How Bot Bench works
        
      
    
        
    
        Build your robot from real LEGO parts, then Test it: drive it with the keyboard or run a program, on a mat and around obstacles you set up. Sizes, weights and motor speeds are the real ones.
        
    
        Or just tell the Assistant (the Ask button, or press /); it lives in the right-hand panel and takes pictures and LEGO files too: "add 3 hubs on top of 3 medium motors", "make the hub black", "drive forward 30 cm then turn right". It asks when something isn't clear. The sun/moon button switches dark and light.
        
    
        
      
        
        
        Build
        
        
        
          
        Add a part
        Click it in the list, then click in the view
        
          
        Move a part
        Drag it; it clicks onto the pin or hole under the cursor
        
          
        Lift up or down
        Shift + drag
        
          
        Place by hand
        Alt + drag, or C to turn auto-connect off
        
          
        Turn 90°
        R · tip over ShiftR
        
          
        Nudge
        ←↑↓→ · up/down PgUpPgDn
        
          
        Select several
        Shift + click · double-click for everything attached
        
          
        Duplicate · delete
        ⌘/CtrlD · Del
        
          
        Undo · redo
        ⌘/CtrlZ · ⌘/CtrlShiftZ
        
          
        Zoom to a part
        F · with nothing selected, Fit shows the whole robot
        
          
        Turn, tip, raise
        The buttons under the view, when a part is selected
        
          
        More room
        [ ] hide the left and right panels (or the buttons at the top right)
        
        
        
      
        
      
        
        
        Test
        
        
        
          
        Play · pause
        Space
        
          
        Reset
        Esc
        
          
        Drive
        WASD or the arrow keys
        
          
        Drive slowly
        Hold Shift for careful lining up
        
          
        Arm motors
        QE · ZC · RF · TG
        
          
        Ports
        Set each motor's port and job in the Drive tab, to match your real robot
        
          
        Start position
        Click the robot while stopped, then drag it
        
          
        Code
        Blocks or SPIKE Python, in the Code tab
